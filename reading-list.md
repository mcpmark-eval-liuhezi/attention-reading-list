# Attention & the Transformer - reading list (draft)

Core paper: "Attention Is All You Need" (Vaswani et al., 2017) - dropped recurrence and convolutions entirely and relied on attention alone.

Claims I jotted down from memory - these need checking:
- It scored roughly 29 BLEU on the WMT 2014 English-to-German translation task.
- The base model had 16 attention heads.
- Training took only a few days on a handful of GPUs.
